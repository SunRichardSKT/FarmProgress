Farm Progress redistributes the unmodified official BepInEx 5.4.23.2 Windows x64 loader package.
Release: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases/tag/v5.4.23.2
BepInEx source: https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/tree/v5.4.23.2
License: MIT (BepInEx.txt).

The loader contains Harmony/HarmonyX, Mono.Cecil and MonoMod components.
Their upstream copyright notices and licenses are included in this directory.
https://github.com/BepInEx/HarmonyX
https://github.com/pardeike/Harmony
https://github.com/jbevain/cecil
https://github.com/MonoMod/MonoMod

winhttp.dll is Unity Doorstop 4.3.0, supplied unmodified in the official BepInEx package.
License: GNU LGPL 2.1 (UnityDoorstop.txt).
Corresponding source release: https://github.com/NeighTools/UnityDoorstop/tree/v4.3.0
Source archive: https://github.com/NeighTools/UnityDoorstop/archive/refs/tags/v4.3.0.zip
Doorstop is a separate replaceable library. You may replace it with a compatible rebuilt library;
Farm Progress imposes no restriction on modifications or debugging of that library.
The upstream source archive is also attached alongside the Farm Progress release.

No Farm Together game assembly or decompiled game source is redistributed.
